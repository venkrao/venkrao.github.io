Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 2026

Memlum Meditation ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This app is designed to work without accounts, cloud sync, or analytics.

Core promise: your meditation session data and app preferences stay on your device.

What We Collect

We do not collect personal data in the app itself.

  • No account information
  • No session history sent to us
  • No analytics or advertising identifiers
  • No recording, camera, or location data

What Is Stored On Device

To make the app usable between launches, it stores a few local preferences such as selected duration and sound toggles. These preferences stay on your phone.

Audio

The meditation drone and ending chime are bundled in the app package and played locally. No streaming is required after install.

Permissions

The release app is configured to avoid camera, microphone, storage, contacts, location, notification, and network-related permissions.

Third-Party Services

We do not use third-party analytics or advertising SDKs for user tracking in the app.
